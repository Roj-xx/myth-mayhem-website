title: "Myth Mayhem — Player's Guide"

Welcome, Inheritor. This guide explains everything you need to play, in
plain words.

> Some features (marked *coming soon*) may not be in every version of the game.

---

## 1. The story so far

Long ago, the human world and the spirit world lived side by side. Fear and
war drove them apart, and people forgot that aswang, diwata and the old gods
were ever real.

Now something has **woken them up**. Only **two people** can summon these
beings: you, and one rival. You both seek an ancient **Relic** that could
change the border between the worlds forever.
- One of you wants to **separate** the worlds to protect humanity.
- The other wants to **control** both worlds to end the chaos.

Neither of you is completely right. You meet in battle on the **Boundary**,
where the two worlds touch.

---

## 2. How to win

You have **100 HP**, and so does your rival.
**Bring your rival's HP to 0 to win.**
If you both reach 0 at the same moment, it's a **draw**.

You can **concede** at any time, which counts as a loss. Online, if you lose
your connection you have **60 seconds** to come back before the match is lost.
The game reconnects by itself, so keep it open and online.

---

## 3. Before the match

### Pick a deck
A deck has **20 cards**. You start with two:

| Deck | Style | Some of its cards |
|---|---|---|
| **Night Hunters** | Fast and aggressive | Aswang, Tiktik, Wakwak, Tiyanak |
| **Forest Guardians** | Tough and defensive | Diwata, Kapre, Duwende, Bantayog |

### Pick a patron God
Your God never fights on the board. It helps you from above with:
- a **Passive** that is always on, and
- an **Active** power that you charge up and then use.

You start with three Gods:

| God | Style | Passive | Active |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adlaw** (sun) | Attack | Swift creatures get +3 ATK the turn they arrive | Deal 3 damage to every enemy creature |
| **Aman Sinaya** (protector of fishermen) | Defense | Guard creatures get +3 HP | A Net: enemy creatures can't attack on their next turn |
| **Bulan** (moon) | Cards | If your hand is empty at the start of your turn, draw 1 | Draw 2 cards |

### Kara y Krus: who goes first
An **agimat coin** decides who goes first.
1. Player 1 (the host, or you against the AI) is always **Kara** (face); Player 2 (the joiner, or the AI) is always **Krus** (cross). Nobody has to call.
2. Both Inheritors are shown with their side, then the coin flips by itself.
3. The side it lands on goes first. It's always a fair 50/50.

If you go second, you get **+1 summon point** to make up for it.

### Your starting hand
After the coin toss you draw **5 cards** (the player who goes second gets **6**, to make up for the first strike). You may swap out **one** of them,
**once**: tap a card, then tap the swap icon (or Confirm); it flips over, is shuffled back into
your deck and you draw a new one in its place. Swapping doesn't start the game: press
**Confirm** (or let the 15 seconds run out) when you're happy. The game starts
once both players have confirmed.

## 4. The battlefield

```
        RIVAL'S INHERITOR (100 HP)
   ┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
   │ Building │ Building │ Building │
   │ Creature │ Creature │ Creature │
   ├──────────┼──── ◆ ───┼──────────┤  ◆ = the shared Domain slot
   │ Creature │ Creature │ Creature │
   │ Building │ Building │ Building │
   └──────────┴──────────┴──────────┘
     Lane 1      Lane 2      Lane 3
          YOUR INHERITOR (100 HP)
```

- There are **3 lanes**.
- In each lane you can have **1 creature** and **1 Building**.
- There's **1 Domain** slot in the middle, shared by both players.

---

## 5. Your turn, step by step

### Step 1: Start of turn (automatic)
- You get **summon points (SP)**. This is your money for playing cards.
  - First turn: **3 SP** (4 if you went second).
  - After that: **+2 SP** every turn.
  - Unused SP stays with you, up to **10**.
- Your God gets **1 Charge**.
- You **draw 1 card**, every turn including your first. You can hold up to
  **10** cards; a card drawn past that is discarded.

### Step 2: Do things (any order)
- **Play cards** by paying their SP cost.
- **Make an Alay** (a sacrifice) if you need more SP. See section 7.
- **Use your God's power** when it has **3 Charges**.

### Step 3: End your turn, and your creatures attack
When you press **End Turn**, your creatures attack **automatically**, one lane
at a time from left to right. In each lane, your creature hits the **first**
thing it finds:

1. An enemy **Bantayog**, which takes the hit and protects everything behind it.
2. An enemy **creature**. It takes the hit. Only a creature with **Guard** hits you back.
3. An enemy **Building**. Flying creatures skip this.
4. **The enemy Inheritor.** This is how you win!

**Remember:**
- **Round 1 is for setting up:** both players' first turns have no attacks at all (place creatures, Alay, or skip). From round 2 every creature attacks, even one you just summoned.
- Only **your** creatures attack on your turn. Your rival's creatures just hit
  back, and they always do, even one that only just arrived.

### Turn timer
Online, each turn has **30 seconds**, plus a small backup reserve for the whole
match. If time runs out, your turn ends, but your creatures still attack.
Running out of time **3 turns in a row** loses the match. Against the AI, only your own
turn is timed (the clock pauses while attacks play). The tutorial has no timer.

## 6. The card types

| Type | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Creature** | A mythical being that fights in a lane | *Kapre*: a tough tree giant that weakens the enemy in front of it |
| **Ritual** | A spell you cast once; some effects last a few turns | *Kulam*: deal 6 damage to an enemy creature |
| **Domain** | Changes the whole battlefield for a few turns | *Enchanted Balete*: heals your creatures every turn |
| **Building** | Sits in a lane for a few turns and helps you | *Bantayog*: blocks every attack in its lane |

**Reading a creature card:**
- **Cost:** how much SP it takes to play.
- **ATK:** how much damage it deals.
- **HP:** how much damage it can take before it dies.
- **Keywords and abilities:** its special powers.
- **Alay line:** what happens if you sacrifice it (only on some cards).

**Rarity** shows how hard a card is to find: ★3 Common, ★4 Rare, ★5 Legendary,
and ✦ Divine for Gods only. Rarity doesn't make a card stronger, just rarer.

## 7. Alay: the art of sacrifice

**Alay** means "offering." It's what makes Myth Mayhem different.

When you need more SP, you can **give something up**:

| You sacrifice… | You get… |
|---|---|
| A card from your hand | **+1 SP** |
| A creature on your board | **SP equal to half its cost** (rounded up) |
| A token (like the Garuda's Malakas and Maganda) | **0 SP**, but it frees the lane |

**Rules:**
- Up to **2 Alay per turn**, and at most **+3 SP** from Alay per turn.
- Your **first** Alay each turn also gives your God **+1 Charge**.
- Some cards have a special **Alay effect** that happens when you sacrifice
  them. For example:
  - **Diwata:** restore 9 HP to your Inheritor.
  - **Tiktik:** draw a card.
  - **Duwende:** gives +2 SP instead of +1.
- Extra-SP bonuses (Duwende, Kapre, Dambana, Agueo) don't add up: each Alay
  uses only the biggest one.
- **Legendary** creatures can't be sacrificed from the board (from your hand is fine).
- **Buildings** can never be sacrificed, not even as a card in your hand.
- Sacrificing a creature isn't the same as it dying, so "when this dies"
  effects don't happen.

**When to use Alay:**
- You need **just a bit more SP** for a big card.
- A creature is **about to die anyway**, so get something for it.
- A lane is **full** and you want to put something better there.
- You have a card you **can't play right now**.

## 8. Keywords

| Keyword | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Swift** | A Guard can't hit it back when it strikes |
| **Flight** | Flies over Buildings to hit the Inheritor (but can't get past Bantayog) |
| **Pierce** | When it attacks and kills a creature, leftover damage goes on to the Building behind it, or to the Inheritor if there's no Building |
| **Drain** | Damage it deals heals your Inheritor, even when it's hitting back |
| **Shield** | Soaks up a set amount of damage, over one or more hits, then breaks |
| **Elusive** | Your rival can't pick it as a target for anything: spells, God powers, Alay effects or abilities |
| **Guard** | Creatures next to it take 3 less damage (the Guard itself doesn't). A Guard also hits back when an enemy creature strikes it; other creatures never do |

**Notes:**
- Damage can only be reduced by **3 per hit** at most, no matter how many
  effects you stack.
- Two copies of the same card don't double an aura: two Torogans still give
  +3 ATK.

## 9. Domains: the fight for the land

Only **one Domain** can be active at a time. It helps **whoever played it**.

Every Domain is one of three kinds, and they beat each other like
rock-paper-scissors:

```
            🌿 Nature
         ▲              │
         │ beats         │ beats
         │              ▼
       👻 Spirit ◄── 🏘 Human
                    beats
```

- **Nature beats Human**: the forest takes back the village.
- **Human beats Spirit**: faith and walls keep ghosts away.
- **Spirit beats Nature**: ghosts haunt the wild.

**When your rival has a Domain active:**

| Your Domain… | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Beats** theirs | Yours replaces it |
| Is the **same** kind | Yours replaces it, but costs **+1 SP** |
| **Loses** to theirs | You have to wait until theirs ends, or sacrifice your card with Alay |

You can always replace **your own** Domain.

## 10. Buildings

- A Building sits in your lane's Building slot.
- It has **HP**, but it **doesn't attack**.
- It only lasts a **few turns**. A countdown on the card shows how many are left.
- You can play a new Building over your own to refresh or swap it.

| Building | Lasts | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bantayog** | 3 turns | Blocks every enemy attack in its lane, even flying ones |
| **Torogan** | 2 turns | All your creatures get +3 ATK |
| **Dambana** | 3 turns | Your first Alay each turn gives +1 extra SP |

---

## 11. While you wait for your rival

You can watch your rival's turn live:
- You'll see each card they play, shown for a moment before it takes effect.
- You'll see their sacrifices, God powers and attacks.
- You can see **how many** cards they hold, but **not which ones**.

While you wait, you can read any card, plan your next turn and send an emote.
Nobody can interrupt another player's turn.

## 12. How a match plays out

| Stage | What usually happens |
|---|---|
| **Early (turns 1–3)** | Cheap creatures fill the lanes. Round 1 has no attacks, so use it to set up your lanes. |
| **Middle (turns 4–7)** | The big choices: sacrifice to play something big, raise a Building for a push, fight over the Domain. |
| **Late (turns 8+)** | Legendaries like Bakunawa and Minokawa, plus God powers, swing the battle. |

**The Boundary Collapse:** if nobody has won by **round 7**, the Boundary
starts to crumble. At the start of every round, **both** Inheritors take
damage, more each round. Matches always end within about 10 minutes.

**Empty deck:** if you need to draw but your deck is empty, you take damage
instead, more each time.

## 14. Collecting cards *(demo in this build)*

> In this build, collecting is a **demo**: open **Deck Builder**, **Card Collection** or **Shop** from the main menu to
> try pulls, packs, crafting and deck building with free demo currency. It's saved on your device and **doesn't change
> your matches**: battles still use the preset decks.

- **Every card has one version.** A card you pull is exactly as strong as one you craft. **Money can't buy you stronger cards.**
- **Tawag (pulling):** each pull gives you **one** card or God.
  - **Gods are the rarest.** You're guaranteed one by your **90th** pull.
  - Each banner features one God.
  - Sometimes a card comes with **special art**.
- **Duplicates** turn into **Abo** (ash). Use Abo to **craft** any card or God.
- **Card packs:** spend **Ginto** on a pack of **5 cards** with at least one Rare. Packs never contain Gods.
- **Other ways to get cards:** a daily **Ginto** shop, story rewards, and buying cards with **Mutya**. Gods can't be bought directly.

| Currency | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Ginto** | Gold you earn by playing |
| **Mutya** | Premium gems |
| **Abo** | Ash from duplicates, used for crafting |

## 16. Quick tips

1. **Don't leave lanes empty.** An empty lane is an open door to your Inheritor.
2. **Alay is a tool, not a must.** Sacrifice when it wins you something now.
3. **Watch the Domain triangle.** Keep a Domain that beats your rival's.
4. **Buildings don't last.** Use Torogan's 2 turns for an all-out push.
5. **Save your God's power** for the moment that swings the fight.
6. **Watch the clock.** From round 7, every round hurts both of you.
