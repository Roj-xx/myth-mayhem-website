## v0.3.0: the full match

**Released:** 22 November 2026 · **Platforms:** Android, Windows

**New**
- Online play across phone and PC, with automatic reconnect if your connection drops.
- A new Kara y Krus: both Inheritors are revealed, then the coin flips by itself.
- One complete tutorial that teaches every mechanic.
- Tap any card to see its cost, stats, skills, Alay effect and a line of lore.
- A Domain clash animation and a guide to the Nature, Human and Spirit triangle.
- The Boundary Collapse: from round 7, both players lose HP every round, so every match ends.

**Better**
- Attack effects for every creature, and an aura on Legendary creatures.
- A bigger Domain slot that glows when a Domain is active.
- Easier tapping: every lane is equally easy to hit.
- New sounds for hits, Rituals, and Victory, Defeat and Draw.
- Layout fixes for wide and tall screens.

**Fixed**
- Click ripples that stayed on screen.
- Creatures floating above or sinking into the board during attacks.

## v0.2.0: steadier online play

**Released:** 29 October 2026 · **Platform:** Android

**New**
- A pause menu with Resume, Concede and Main Menu.
- A Draw result screen.
- A "Tap screen to skip" hint during cinematics.
- The official app icon and splash screen.

**Better**
- Clear messages when an online connection fails, instead of waiting forever.
- You can retry joining or creating a room after an error.
- Players on different game versions can no longer join the same match.
- Graphics settings tuned for phones.

**Fixed**
- If your opponent leaves mid-match, you now get the win.
- The game returns safely to the menu if a match fails to load.

---

## v0.1.0: first playable

**Released:** 23 October 2026 · **Platform:** Android

**New**
- The first Android build of Myth Mayhem.
- A battlefield with lanes for each side.
- The first creatures from Philippine folklore, including the Aswang, Tikbalang and Manananggal.
- Basic creature combat, with HP for each player.
